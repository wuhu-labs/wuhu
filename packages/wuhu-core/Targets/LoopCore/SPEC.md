# LoopCore

## Service ownership

`SessionExecution` is the sessions' runtime and the one thing the routes hold: it builds the scripts and the tool executor, and runs the loops on the space's worker (`Space.worker`: wuwei's shared backend over the space's own database). Nothing runs before `start`, which gives that worker the loops' kinds and their `LoopClient` and starts it (mail waits in the store, so main's API can be bound to the tools first; a second `start` does nothing), or after `shutdown`, which stops that worker. Session cells are therefore shared-backend only. Its verbs check what a verb needs before mailing it (existence, busy and archived refusals; permission is the routes'); the cell makes every write of a verb.

Every input is mail and returns once it is durable; nothing waits for the cell, so a verb's reply is computed by main, not read back. An input reaches the queue only through the cell: `put` queues it (on a topic it replaces the unseen input there; a `throttle` makes it due no sooner than that long after the model last saw the topic) and `clear` takes back the unseen input on a topic, each then mailing the cell a `look`, a step of its own. Main sends both as `SessionMail` rows in the transaction of whatever caused them (a post, a request, an observation delivery, a timer, a cache-warming notice, an exec or script notice) and pokes the cell once that commits; mail nobody poked for is found by the worker's scan, which is also all a boot does. Until the cell has taken a `put`, the session's `work` does not show it. `look` has the cell look at its tables and tells the work at hand when to yield to what waits. `retitle` and `retag` write the record. `compact` writes the compact command and looks. `interrupt` writes the hold on a live, unheld, unerrored kernel session and stops its job. `resume` clears the hold or error, counts the resume and advances. `archive` stops the session's scripts, then the cell stops its job, marking the session interrupted if one was working, archives it and invalidates inference. `unarchive` and `restart` write, invalidate inference and advance; a restart finding a job working (one started after main's check) is dropped, and queued input waits for the new generation. `cancel` stops the job and nothing else (a resume that could not read the session marked it errored). A verb the cell finds invalid (unknown session, grace expired) throws: its write rolls back and the mail is consumed. Archive (unless forced) and restart first read that the session is settled: no pending compact command, and the session stopped (interrupted, errored, not live) or with no mail on its way to its cell, nothing to do and no job working. Resume refuses unreadable stored data with `SessionError.unreadableData(id)`, whose description directs the caller to Start over.

## The session cell

`SessionCell` (id = session id) has no state beyond the session tables: every call hydrates the session, applies what it brings and advances it, writing through `SessionRows` on the cell step's own connection (the host's GRDB `Database`). What the session works on is the job named by the transcript's end, its slot (generation, position): `<s>:<g>:<p>` for a `CompactionJob` and a `ToolCallJob`, `<s>:<g>:<p>:<stepTry>` for a normal `InferenceJob` and the same with `:compact` for a forced one. A job that exists works, except a step that ended cancelled or parked. A job's answer (`stepped`, `called`, `compacted`) for a slot the transcript has left, or for a session no longer live, is dropped.

Advancing writes every step that needs no job and stops at the first that does: a pending fold runs only on a delivery; then the next pending tool call (kernel tools `bookmark` and `compact(park:)` are written at once, `compact` starts a fold, any other starts a `ToolCallJob`); then a queue drain; then a pending compact command (instructions materialized into the transcript, a forced step); then work (the generation head's next re-establishing read, else a normal step); then, after a model stop, a nag. A nag due when the model stops is written with its reply, so the session never reads settled while it owes one. The work at hand learns when to give the turn back by mail, `yield(by:)`: a normal step at once for a pending compact command, a normal step or a tool call by the earliest grace of the due input it has not seen (from when that input came due: a person's message to the session itself 10 s, anything else 90 s, the session's own context deliveries none); a tool call started while input waits has it in its params. A forced step and a compaction never yield. An input can be delayed (`due_at`) or put on a topic, which replaces the unseen input on that topic; until it is due it is not drained and does not count as work, The cell's `nextWake()` is the earlier of the next delayed input's due time and, for a model that stopped with nothing to do, its next park reminder (`Environment.nextTimer`); its `wake()` is a look.

The cell also keeps the turn in a row of its own, `session_state` (`SessionTurn`: generation, position, `turn` = `inert` / `model` / `tools(calls)` / `idle` / `folding(call)`, the head's unanswered `reestablish` reads, `replied`), moved in the step that writes the transcript: a reply, the answer to a call, input after the tail, a new generation. The cell does not read it yet. Each advance compares it with what the transcript folds to: a row that differs is replaced and reported to `LoopConfig.turnDifference`, and a session without a row gets the fold.

## Inference

An inference step is an `InferenceJob`. It reads exactly the transcript its id names (`SessionStore.transcript(session, generation:, count:)`) before every attempt and owns the attempts, their records, the waits between them and the outcome; a wait is kept as an absolute `retryAt` with its jitter, so a restart waits out the rest of it. Every outcome ends the job, and a step that already ended answers again with that outcome, so a reply is never paid for twice. A resume increments `stepTry`, so the step after it is a fresh job.

A normal step first checks the compaction thresholds: at or past hard it answers `full` and the cell starts a forced step; past soft with no pending pressure notice it answers `full` and the cell writes one context notice with its percentage, which retries and restarts reuse. A reply carries its fullness: a model that stopped past the hard threshold is compacted before anything else. A forced reply without a `compact` call, a forced step that fails, and `contextTooLong` fall back to the mechanical compactor; so does `requestTooLarge`, except in a generation past the first with no reply of its own yet, where the session errors with `requestTooLargeAfterCompaction(limitBytes:)`. `capacityExceeded` is handled as `requestTooLarge`.

`InferenceReply.committed` receives the assistant entry (provider ids replaced by kernel ids, their map kept) and the transcript with it before the job answers; the cell then writes that entry verbatim. It is never invoked for a failed or cancelled attempt.

An outage (a throttle or an unreachable network) retries without bound. A throttle that carries `retryAt` (from `Retry-After`, resolved against the injected `\.date`) waits until that instant, at most one hour, drawing no jitter; a `retryAt` not in the future counts as absent and backs off exponentially with full jitter up to 300 s. Idle timeouts escalate 120 s, 300 s, 900 s and park after the third in a row; other unretryable failures park after eight. `streamCut` uses the bounded transient schedule. Waits run on the injected clock and end on `interrupt` or at the step's yield deadline.

## Tool calls and compaction

A tool call is a `ToolCallJob`: it runs the call, has an exec or a script answer `running` once its yield deadline passes, and answers its result. A second start of the same call (a restart) answers `interrupted`, which the cell writes as the interrupted-tool failure; it is never rerun.

A `CompactionJob` folds the generation its id names: the model's `compact` call (`fold`), the mechanical compactor (`fallback`) or a portable handoff (`handoff`). It gathers what lies outside the session (running work, the requests the session awaits with when each task last reported, placement through `prepareCompaction`, open requests) into the next generation's head; the cell writes it, ends a pending compact command and invalidates inference. A compaction that fails errors the session.

The kernel `compact` tool refuses an empty or whitespace-only summary with `CompactArgumentsError.emptySummary`, as an ordinary tool failure; pre-reads do not waive it. Its optional `model` (provider/model or a model group id) and `effort` name the target the next generation continues on; an empty model (`emptyModel`) or an effort without a model (`effortWithoutModel`) fails the same way, and a target `prepareCompaction` refuses is a tool failure naming it: nothing folds and the turn continues. With `park: true` (not with `model`: `parkWithModel`) the tool writes only its closing result and the fold is pending (`Transcript.phase` is `.foldPending`): no work, pending calls, nags or compaction. Park is refused with `ParkRefusal` while the session owes an owed reply (`owesReply`) or an open request's final report (`owesReport`). A compact command on a pending fold completes with nothing to do; the next delivery runs the fold first, so the delivered input lands in the next generation.

`ReplayBoundaryRequired` ends a step with `handoff`: the cell starts a handoff compaction, which projects a portable transcript into a new generation and binding; the next step resolves again. A forced compaction whose inference meets `ModelRouteWaiting` or a rate limit folds mechanically instead of waiting, so `prepareCompaction` can move the next generation to a member with room.

## Streams

A session's `url` observation without a handler is a `StreamJob` the cell creates on `stream` mail at `<session>:<subscription>`, its row in the job's `streams` table (url, armed at, last event id), which the `subscriptions` view, Start over's `<session-state>` and `cancel` read. The job dials `LoopConfig.sources` with `Last-Event-ID` set to the last event its owner acked, and sends its owner one event at a time as `Streamed.streamed`, typed by a retyped `Recipient`: the next only after `acked`. The cell appends the event to the unseen input on the subscription's topic (the first one due no sooner than the throttle past the last the model saw) and acks at once. A drop, a 408/429 or a 5xx redials with backoff from 1 s to 5 min, at least `Retry-After`; any other answer deletes the row and sends one ending notice. `unstream` mail (the gate's after `cancel`) and `archive` mail `stop` to the job, which deletes its row; an event that finds the session archived stops it too.

## Removed executor

The loop runs only kernel sessions. A stored executor other than `kernel` is retired: its cell queues what it is sent and runs nothing, interrupt is a no-op, resume and unarchive are refused with `ExecutorUnavailableError` ("executor no longer supported"), and its transcript reads empty. Start over onto a kernel provider preserves identity and retained queue/subscriptions.

## Archive

`SessionExecution.archive(id, force:)` walks the live sessions of the parent-chain subtree, best effort; only the root's archive rights are checked. Without force, every one must be settled (`revoke(.whenSettled)`; retired sessions are never busy), else nothing is mailed and `SubtreeArchiveBusy` names every busy session by id and title. Then, for each, main closes its open requests with a final-kind message saying the session was archived before reporting, trims its slots (retiring ones and handler firings) and mails `archive`, which also stops its streams. A session that turned busy after the check is archived anyway, as with force; one created during the walk is missed.

## Park reminders

The space's worker runs on SpaceCore's `DependencyScheduler`: wuwei's live scheduler on the injected `\.date` and `\.continuousClock`, so `client.now()`, wuwei's timers and the store read one clock and a test that moves time moves all three. A stopgap until the store and main run on wuwei.

## Telemetry

The loop emits no spans of its own; `model.attempt` spans come from the inference closure, with the cache expectation the transcript's last reply predicts.

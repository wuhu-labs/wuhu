Privacy Policy

How Wuhu handles information across your device, your space, and connected services.

Effective September 20, 2026

Who we are

Wuhu is developed by Hangzhou Hu Di Shen Shan Technology Co., Ltd. (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Wuhu app, wuhu.ai, our notification relay, support, and demonstration spaces we operate. For privacy questions or requests, contact lms@xnzg.tech.

Wuhu connects to an independently hosted space chosen by you or your organization. The space operator controls its server, membership, content, agents, integrations, backups, and retention. We do not receive the contents of every Wuhu space merely because we develop the app. An independent operator’s privacy practices also apply to the space you join.

What your space receives

When you enroll a device, the space receives a public authentication key, an installation identifier, device information, and the account association needed to recognize and authorize it. The app keeps its private signing key in the device’s Keychain.

The space stores the content you choose to send or save: messages, documents, tables, tasks, selected images and attachments, and your profile name, handle, and avatar. It also maintains revisions, conversation and task history, and operational records. Your content can be available to the space operator and other people or agents with access to the space. Choose your space and what you share accordingly.

AI providers and connected tools

AI work runs through the providers and agent executors configured by the space operator. To answer a message or carry out a task, the space or its executor may send the conversation, attachments, relevant space documents, instructions, and tool results to an external model provider. Configurations can use providers such as OpenAI (including Codex), Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, or another compatible service. The actual recipients depend on the space and the selected agent or model.

Agents may read or change space content and use connected machines or external tools according to the permissions and configuration the operator supplies. Those tools can send data to their own service providers. Ask the space operator which services are enabled before sharing personal or confidential information. Each provider’s terms, retention, and data-use practices apply; self-hosting Wuhu does not mean inference always happens locally.

If you choose dictation, the app records your speech after microphone permission and sends the recording to your space for transcription by its configured provider. The returned text appears in the composer. The transcription provider can differ from the provider used for the conversation.

Notifications

On supported devices, Wuhu uses our notification relay at notifications.wuhu.ai, hosted on Cloudflare, and Apple Push Notification service. The relay stores the Apple push token, platform and environment, and opaque subscription identifiers and credential hashes needed to route notifications.

The space sends notification text, event and session identifiers, and delivery metadata through the relay to Apple. The standard server uses generic alert text rather than message previews, but the relay processes the payload supplied by the space. These payloads are protected in transit with TLS and are readable by the relay; they are not end-to-end encrypted. You can control notification permission in your device’s system settings.

Website, support, and diagnostics

Our website is served through Cloudflare. Network requests necessarily expose connection information such as an IP address and requested URL to the service handling them. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website, and the app does not include an advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.

If you email lms@xnzg.tech, we receive your address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use this to respond and troubleshoot. The app writes operational logs on your device; a space and its hosting providers may also keep server and connection logs. If you share diagnostic material with us, we use it to investigate your report. Please remove private content and credentials first.

Why data is used and shared

Data is used to authenticate devices, provide the workspace and requested AI features, deliver notifications, maintain security and reliability, and answer support requests. We do not sell personal information, serve targeted advertising, or use app data to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Data may be processed by the hosting, notification, email, and AI providers needed for those functions, or disclosed when required by law or to address abuse or security incidents. An independently operated space may have its own service providers. Processing can occur outside your country, depending on where the space and these providers operate.

Retention and deletion

Space content: the operator controls retention and backups. Deleting a visible item may leave revisions, conversation history, or backups. Leaving a space, logging out, or uninstalling the app does not erase its server-side content. Ask the operator to remove your account, revoke devices, or delete content and retained copies. For a demonstration space we operate, contact lms@xnzg.tech.

Notification relay: active subscriptions expire seven days after their last renewal. Notification delivery records become unavailable after seven days; physical cleanup occurs during later processing and may take longer for inactive destinations. Device destination records and security bindings do not currently have an automatic deletion deadline. Contact us for a privacy request concerning these records. Withdrawing a subscription or disabling notifications stops or limits delivery, but is not a promise that all relay records are immediately deleted.

Support: we keep correspondence while handling the request and for follow-up, security, and any applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete it. Model and tool providers control the retention of information they receive under their own terms.

Your choices and rights

You can choose which space to join, what to send, whether to use dictation, and which images to select. The app uses system pickers for selected photos and files; it does not need access to your entire photo library to attach an image. You can change microphone and notification permissions in system settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. For data held by us, email lms@xnzg.tech; we may need to verify your request. For an independent space, contact its operator. We can help identify the appropriate contact but cannot access or erase a server we do not operate.

Children and updates

Wuhu is a general-purpose workspace and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to a service we operate without appropriate permission, contact lms@xnzg.tech.

We will update this page when our practices change and show the effective date above. Material changes that require notice or permission will be handled as required by applicable law.

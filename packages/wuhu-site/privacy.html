Privacy Policy

How Wuhu handles information across your device, your space, and connected services.

Effective October 8, 2026

Who we are

Wuhu is developed by Hangzhou Hu Di Shen Shan Technology Co., Ltd. (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Wuhu app, wuhu.ai, our hosted Wuhu service, our notification relay, support, and demonstration spaces we operate. For privacy questions or requests, contact lms@xnzg.tech.

Wuhu connects to a space chosen by you or your organization, either independently hosted or hosted by us. The space operator controls its server, membership, content, agents, integrations, backups, and retention. We do not receive the contents of every Wuhu space merely because we develop the app. An independent operator’s privacy practices also apply to the space you join.

Hosted Wuhu

Our hosted service gives you your own workspace at <name>.wuhu.space. You must be at least 18 to use it. The following describes the data we handle when you use a hosted account through wuhu.ai or the Wuhu apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.

  • Account: we keep your email address and the invite code you used. Sign-in is passwordless: we email you a one-time link or code, and the tokens behind it expire shortly after they are sent and cannot be reused. If you join the waitlist, we keep that email address too.
  • Workspace: sessions, messages, files, tables, pages, and what your agents produce live in your own workspace server. We store them so the service can work. We do not look at them except for support you ask for or where the law requires it, and we do not use your workspace or prompts to train AI models.
  • Model usage: for each AI request, we record usage and billing metadata per workspace: the model, token counts, cost, status, and time. We use this to enforce the free trial budget. Our metering proxy forwards requests and records this metadata, never prompts or completions; the usage ledger does not keep the content of your prompts or the model’s replies.
  • Logs: our servers and Cloudflare log IP addresses and the addresses of requested pages, never request contents. We use these logs for operation, troubleshooting, security, and abuse prevention. wuhu.ai keeps them for 7 days, and workspace hosts for up to 30 days.
  • Apps and notifications: the apps talk to your workspace directly. If you enable notifications, your device’s push token is stored so notifications can reach you. A hosted notification carries the text of the message it is about (up to about 2 KB), sent through our notification relay to Apple’s push service. The relay keeps queued notifications for about 7 days. Crash reports you choose to share with developers in iOS settings come to us through Apple.

AI providers: agents send text and files from your workspace to an AI model. The apps ask for your permission before this happens, and you can withdraw it in the Settings menu under AI sharing. During the trial, the default provider is DeepSeek: requests go through our metering proxy to DeepSeek’s API. DeepSeek is based in the People’s Republic of China, and what you send is processed under its own terms and privacy policy, which may include storage on servers in China. Do not put anything into the trial you are not comfortable sending there. We may offer other models, such as Anthropic’s Claude via OpenRouter; if your workspace uses one, your requests go to that provider instead, under its terms. If you configure your own AI provider in your workspace, those requests go straight to that provider under its terms. We don't meter or see that traffic. Dictation isn't available on hosted workspaces.

Hosting and processors: your workspace runs on Amazon Web Services in Oregon, USA (us-west-2). Sign-up, sign-in, and waitlist data is held on Cloudflare Workers and D1, and sign-in emails are sent through Cloudflare. Cloudflare also provides our website, DNS, and notification relay. DeepSeek processes trial AI requests; OpenRouter and the model provider behind it process requests only if your workspace uses that preset. Apple provides app distribution, push notifications, and crash reports you choose to share. If you are outside the US, your data is transferred to the US, and to China when you use DeepSeek.

Retention:

  • We keep your workspace until you delete your account. Deletion removes it from our servers at once; any copy made for maintenance or recovery is gone within 30 days. The usage ledger is deleted with your workspace.
  • We keep your email address until you delete your account, then remove it from our systems within 30 days. We retain one record of the workspace name and deletion time to show that deletion happened and prevent the name from being reused by mistake. Our email provider keeps copies of sent sign-in emails for a short time and may keep addresses that bounced on its do-not-send list.
  • We keep your waitlist email until you sign up or ask us to remove it.

Deleting your account: request deletion at wuhu.ai. We email you a confirmation link or code; once you confirm, we delete your workspace, all its data and its server, and its address. This cannot be undone. You can also start deletion from the app: Settings menu, Delete account.

Each hosted workspace runs in its own isolated container with its own storage and network rules, so one workspace cannot reach another. Connections are encrypted. No system is perfect; if something goes wrong that affects your data, we will tell you.

What your space receives

When you enroll a device, the space receives a public authentication key, an installation identifier, device information, and the account association needed to recognize and authorize it. The app keeps its private signing key in the device’s Keychain.

The space stores the content you choose to send or save: messages, documents, tables, tasks, selected images and attachments, and your profile name, handle, and avatar. It also maintains revisions, conversation and task history, and operational records. Your content can be available to the space operator and other people or agents with access to the space. Choose your space and what you share accordingly.

AI providers and connected tools

AI work runs through the providers and agent executors configured by the space operator. To answer a message or carry out a task, the space or its executor may send the conversation, attachments, relevant space documents, instructions, and tool results to an external model provider. Configurations can use providers such as OpenAI (including Codex), Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, or another compatible service. The actual recipients depend on the space and the selected agent or model.

Agents may read or change space content and use connected machines or external tools according to the permissions and configuration the operator supplies. Those tools can send data to their own service providers. Ask the space operator which services are enabled before sharing personal or confidential information. Each provider’s terms, retention, and data-use practices apply; self-hosting Wuhu does not mean inference always happens locally.

If you choose dictation, the app records your speech after microphone permission and sends the recording to your space for transcription by its configured provider. The returned text appears in the composer. The transcription provider can differ from the provider used for the conversation.

Notifications

On supported devices, Wuhu uses our notification relay at notifications.wuhu.ai, hosted on Cloudflare, and Apple Push Notification service. The relay stores the Apple push token, platform and environment, and opaque subscription identifiers and credential hashes needed to route notifications.

The space sends notification text, event and session identifiers, and delivery metadata through the relay to Apple. The relay processes the payload supplied by the space, including message text for hosted Wuhu notifications as described above. These payloads are protected in transit with TLS and are readable by the relay; they are not end-to-end encrypted. You can control notification permission in your device’s system settings.

Website, support, and diagnostics

Our website is served through Cloudflare. Network requests necessarily expose connection information such as an IP address and requested URL to the service handling them. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website, and the app does not include an advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.

If you email lms@xnzg.tech, we receive your address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use this to respond and troubleshoot. The app writes operational logs on your device; a space and its hosting providers may also keep server and connection logs. If you share diagnostic material with us, we use it to investigate your report. Please remove private content and credentials first.

Why data is used and shared

Data is used to authenticate devices, provide the workspace and requested AI features, deliver notifications, maintain security and reliability, and answer support requests. We do not sell personal information, serve targeted advertising, or use app data to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Data may be processed by the hosting, notification, email, and AI providers needed for those functions, or disclosed when required by law or to address abuse or security incidents. An independently operated space may have its own service providers. Processing can occur outside your country, depending on where the space and these providers operate.

Retention and deletion

Independently hosted space content: the operator controls retention and backups. For our hosted service, the account deletion and retention rules in the Hosted Wuhu section above apply. Deleting a visible item may leave revisions, conversation history, or backups. Leaving a space, logging out, or uninstalling the app does not erase its server-side content. Ask the operator to remove your account, revoke devices, or delete content and retained copies. For a demonstration space we operate, contact lms@xnzg.tech.

Notification relay: active subscriptions expire seven days after their last renewal. Notification delivery records become unavailable after seven days; physical cleanup occurs during later processing and may take longer for inactive destinations. Device destination records and security bindings do not currently have an automatic deletion deadline. Contact us for a privacy request concerning these records. Withdrawing a subscription or disabling notifications stops or limits delivery, but is not a promise that all relay records are immediately deleted.

Support: we keep correspondence while handling the request and for follow-up, security, and any applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete it. Model and tool providers control the retention of information they receive under their own terms.

Your choices and rights

You can choose which space to join, what to send, whether to use dictation, and which images to select. The app uses system pickers for selected photos and files; it does not need access to your entire photo library to attach an image. You can change microphone and notification permissions in system settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. For data held by us, email lms@xnzg.tech; we may need to verify your request. For an independent space, contact its operator. We can help identify the appropriate contact but cannot access or erase a server we do not operate.

Children and updates

Wuhu is a general-purpose workspace and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to a service we operate without appropriate permission, contact lms@xnzg.tech.

We will update this page when our practices change and show the effective date above. Material changes that require notice or permission will be handled as required by applicable law.
